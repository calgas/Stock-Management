What it does

  • Keeps raw material (RM), finished goods (FG) and Trade stock, item by item, with live balances and Low / Critical alerts.
  • Records every receipt, issue, production, dispatch, purchase, return, scrap and adjustment in a ledger that is never edited away: mistakes are reversed, not deleted.
  • Shows each item’s full history, with invoices and photos attached to entries.
  • Sends a daily stock workbook by email, and closes the books each financial year into an archive.
  • Gives each person the access their job needs, set in Team & Access.

How it works

The data lives in the company’s own Google Sheet, and a Google Apps Script backend checks and records every change. This app is a web app you can install on a computer or phone; it remembers recent figures, so it opens quickly.

Orders & Dispatch and Batch Card Tracker use the same sign-in.
